HOW TO PLAY

Ultimate high-stakes strategy game based on the classic playground cricket.

Objective

Score more runs than your opponent by choosing numbers from 1 to 6.

Step 1 - Toss

  1. Choose Odd or Even.
  2. Select a number from 1 to 6.
  3. The computer also selects a number.
  4. Add both numbers.
  5. If the result matches your Odd/Even choice, you win the toss.
  6. The toss winner chooses to Bat or Bowl first.

Step 2 - Batting

  • Select a number from 1 to 6.
  • The computer also selects a number.
  • If the numbers are different, your selected number is added to your score.
  • If both numbers are the same, you are OUT.
  • Your innings ends after getting OUT.

Step 3 - Bowling

  • The computer becomes the batsman.
  • You select a number from 1 to 6.
  • The computer selects a number.
  • If the numbers are different, the computer scores its chosen number.
  • If both numbers are the same, the computer is OUT.

Winning

  • The player with the higher score wins.
  • If the second batting player reaches the target, the match ends immediately.
  • If the batting player gets OUT before reaching the target, the bowling player wins.

Rules Summary

  • Choose numbers from 1 to 6.
  • Different numbers = Runs scored.
  • Same numbers = OUT.
  • Two innings are played.
  • The higher score wins the match.
