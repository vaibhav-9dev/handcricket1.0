How To Play

Master the rules, outsmart the CPU, and dominate the digital stadium.

smart_button Game Controls & Buttons Explained

casino Toss Buttons (Odd / Even)

Used at match setup. Pick your call, then choose a number from 1 to 6 to determine who bats or bowls first.

filter_6 Number Keys (1 through 6)

Your main gameplay inputs. Selected during every ball while Batting or Bowling to add runs or force a wicket.

refresh Play Again

Resets the match state in localStorage and immediately takes you back to the toss screen for a rematch.

share Share Score

Uses Web Share API or Clipboard fallback to easily brag about your highest runs on social apps.

Phase 1

poker_chip The Toss

  • • Choose Odd or Even.
  • • Select a number from 1 to 6.
  • • Sum of your pick + CPU pick determines toss winner.
Phase 2

sports_cricket Batting

  • • Tap a number from 1 to 6.
  • • Different numbers = Runs added to your score.
  • • Same number = OUT!
Phase 3

sports_baseball Bowling

  • • Try to guess CPU's input (1 to 6).
  • • Match CPU's number to bowl them OUT.
  • • Keep CPU's total score lower than yours.
